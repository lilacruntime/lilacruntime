### ✧ a little about me

I'm a UK-based developer with a background in **visual effects, game design and animation**. During my VFX degree, I gravitated towards writing **Python tools for artists** — and I've kept building things ever since.

My projects range from **C# applications and game plugins** to **Rust terminal tools** and **custom Linux interfaces**. I also teach programming and game development, which has made me pretty good at translating complicated technical ideas into something approachable.

I care about practical problem-solving, maintainable software and interfaces that feel good to use.[^sparkle]

> [!NOTE]
> This is a new home for my development work. I'm gradually moving selected projects here and improving their documentation. Published projects are linked below; more work is on the way.
